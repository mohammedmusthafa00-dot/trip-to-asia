Why travellers from Madurai choose Thailand

Travellers from Madurai grow up around temples, so Thailand's temples, from Wat Arun in Bangkok to the old city of Chiang Mai, often become the favourite part of the trip.

A Tamil-speaking guide can explain the history and stories behind each temple, which matters most when parents and grandparents are travelling. Every Trip to Asia package includes a 3-star hotel stay, airport transfers, entry fees and Indian food (3 meals). Tamil-guide tours run on the packages where listed, with a minimum of 4 members.

Good to know

Thailand is 1 hour 30 minutes ahead of India, so there is little jet lag. The cool and dry season from November to February is the most comfortable time for temple visits.
