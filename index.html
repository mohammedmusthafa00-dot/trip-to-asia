    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers about our Thailand packages.

    What is included in the Thailand packages?

    Every package includes a 3-star hotel stay, airport transfers, entry fees and Indian food (3 meals). Sightseeing depends on the package, for example Safari World and the Floating Market in Bangkok Escape, or an island stay and snorkelling in Phuket + Krabi Islands.

    How many days are the packages?

    Bangkok Escape is 3 nights / 4 days, Bangkok + Pattaya is 4 nights / 5 days, Bangkok + Chiang Mai is 5 nights / 6 days, Bangkok + Pattaya + Phuket is 6 nights / 7 days, and Phuket + Krabi Islands is 5 nights / 6 days.

    Is a Tamil-speaking guide available?

    Yes. City and island tours with a Tamil guide are included in the packages where listed, with a minimum of 4 members.

    Can I get a custom Thailand itinerary?

    Yes. Send your destinations, travel dates and number of travellers (up to 10) on the Custom Itinerary page, and we will get back to you with a plan and a quote.

    How much do the packages cost?

    The current price is shown on each package card. Prices are marked * because they are not yet confirmed, so send an enquiry for the final quote.

    Do I need a visa for Thailand?

    Visa rules for Indian passport holders can change, so check the latest requirements on the official Thailand e-Visa website or with the Royal Thai Embassy before you book.

    How do I enquire or book?

    Fill in the enquiry form below or message us on WhatsApp at +91 96776 45010.
