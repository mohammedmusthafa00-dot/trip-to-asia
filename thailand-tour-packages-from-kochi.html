Why travellers from Kochi choose Thailand

Travellers from Kochi already know good beaches, so many choose Thailand for something different: limestone islands, clear water and snorkelling on the Andaman Sea.

Our Phuket + Krabi Islands package (5 nights / 6 days) includes an islands tour, a night on an island, snorkelling or diving, the Walking Street market and a local seafood buffet. If you would like a city and beach mix, Bangkok + Pattaya + Phuket covers 6 nights / 7 days. Every package includes a 3-star hotel stay, airport transfers, entry fees and Indian food (3 meals).

Language on tour

Our guided tours are led by a Tamil-speaking guide. If you have language needs for your group, mention them when you enquire and we will confirm what is possible.

Flights and planning from Kochi

Direct flights operate between Kochi and Bangkok on several airlines, so most trips can start with a non-stop flight of around four hours. Schedules change through the year, so send your dates when you enquire and we will fit the package around them.

You can also send a custom itinerary request with your own destinations and dates, or read our guides to Bangkok, Pattaya and Phuket.
