Why travellers from Hyderabad choose Thailand

Hyderabad families tend to look for a holiday that works for children, parents and grandparents at once. Thailand does this well: sights that are easy to enjoy, food that is familiar, and transfers handled for you.

Bangkok and Pattaya are the most popular choice for family trips. Bangkok Escape includes Safari World, Sea Life and the Floating Market, which are easy days out for children. Bangkok + Pattaya adds a one-day island trip and Walking Street in the evening. Every package includes a 3-star hotel, airport transfers, entry fees and Indian food (3 meals).

Travelling with parents or a large family

Because transfers and entry fees are included, there is less to organise on the ground. If your group is larger or needs a different route, use the Custom Itinerary form for up to 10 travellers.
