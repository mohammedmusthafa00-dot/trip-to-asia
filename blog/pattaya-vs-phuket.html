Pattaya or Phuket: which beach destination is right for you?

Both are popular with Indian travellers, but they suit different trips. Here is a simple comparison.

Get a quote

Published 30 September 2026 · by the Trip to Asia team

Pattaya is an easy add-on to Bangkok on the Gulf coast. Phuket is a large island on the Andaman coast, with beaches and boat trips. Your dates and trip length usually decide.

Pattaya vs Phuket at a glance

PattayaPhuket
CoastGulf of ThailandAndaman Sea
Getting thereRoughly two hours by road from BangkokAn island, usually reached by a flight from Bangkok
Best monthsNovember to FebruaryRoughly November to April
AtmosphereLively: beach city, Walking Street, easy day toursSpread-out beaches, old town, island hopping
Best forA short trip combined with BangkokA longer trip focused on beaches and islands
Our packagesBangkok + Pattaya (4N / 5D)Bangkok + Pattaya + Phuket (6N / 7D) or Phuket + Krabi Islands (5N / 6D)

Choose Pattaya if

  • You have 4 to 5 days and want beach time without a long journey from Bangkok.
  • You enjoy a livelier evening scene such as Walking Street.
  • You want an easy island day trip, such as Koh Larn.

Choose Phuket if

  • You have a week or more.
  • You want clear Andaman water, snorkelling and island trips.
  • You are travelling between November and April, when the sea is calmest.

Frequently asked questions

Is Pattaya or Phuket better for beaches?

Phuket and the Andaman coast have clearer water and island trips, especially from November to April. Pattaya is easier to combine with a short Bangkok trip.
