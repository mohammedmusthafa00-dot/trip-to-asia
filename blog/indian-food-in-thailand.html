Indian food in Thailand: what to expect on your trip

Food is one of the first worries for Indian travellers. Here is how it works on our packages, and how to manage on your own.

Get a quote

Published 30 September 2026 · by the Trip to Asia team

You can eat well in Thailand, even if you prefer Indian food or are vegetarian. A little planning makes the difference.

Indian food on our packages

Every Trip to Asia package includes Indian food (3 meals) during the trip. Meals are arranged as part of the itinerary, so you are not searching for a restaurant after a long day of sightseeing. If you have dietary needs, tell us when you enquire so we can plan for them.

Finding Indian food on your own

  • Bangkok has a number of Indian restaurants, including around Phahurat, the area known as Little India.
  • Pattaya and Phuket have Indian restaurants in the main tourist areas.
  • Ask your hotel or guide for a current recommendation, as places open and close.

Eating vegetarian in Thailand

  • Look for food marked jay, the Thai term for strict vegetarian food.
  • Say gin jay (I eat vegetarian food) or ask for food without meat and fish sauce.
  • Fish sauce and shrimp paste are in many dishes, so always ask.
  • Fruit, fresh juices, plain rice and vegetable stir-fries are easy, safe choices.

Thai dishes Indian travellers often enjoy

  • Pad thai (ask for the version without egg or shrimp if needed).
  • Mango with sticky rice.
  • Fresh fruit shakes and coconut water.

Frequently asked questions

Is Indian food available in Thailand?

Yes. Bangkok, Pattaya and Phuket have Indian restaurants, and every Trip to Asia package includes Indian food (3 meals).

Can vegetarians eat well in Thailand?

Yes, with care. Look for food marked jay, say gin jay, and ask about fish sauce and shrimp paste.

What should I tell you about my diet?

Tell us when you enquire about any vegetarian, allergy or other dietary needs so we can plan your meals.
