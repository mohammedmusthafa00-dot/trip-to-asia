Thailand trip cost from India: what to budget

The total cost depends on flights, hotel level, trip length and how much you plan to do. Here is what to count, and where a package can save you money.

Get a quote

Published 30 September 2026 · by the Trip to Asia team

A Thailand trip from India is made up of a few clear costs. Knowing them helps you compare quotes properly and avoid surprise extras.

The main costs of a Thailand trip

  • Flights: usually the biggest variable. Fares change with the season, the airline and how early you book.
  • Hotels: cost depends on the star rating, the location and the number of nights.
  • Airport and city transfers: easy to forget, and costly if you pay for each taxi separately.
  • Sightseeing and entry fees: temples, parks, island trips and shows usually charge separately.
  • Food: street food and local restaurants are cheap; hotel restaurants cost more.
  • Visa, insurance and shopping: check the latest visa rules on the official Thailand e-Visa website before you book.

How to keep the cost down

  • Choose an all-in package so transfers, entry fees and meals are already covered.
  • Travel as a group. Shared transport and guided tours work out cheaper per person.
  • Book early and avoid the busiest holiday dates, when flights and hotels cost the most.
  • Pick a shorter trip first. A 3-night Bangkok Escape is the most affordable way to see Thailand.
