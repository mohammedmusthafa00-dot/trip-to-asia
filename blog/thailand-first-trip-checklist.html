Thailand first trip checklist for Indian travellers

Sort the basics before you fly: documents, money, phone, power plugs, packing and temple manners.

Get a quote

Published 30 September 2026 · by the Trip to Asia team

A little preparation makes a first Thailand trip much smoother. Use this checklist a few weeks before you travel.

Documents

  • Check that your passport is valid for the period the airline and Thai authorities require.
  • Check the latest entry and visa rules on the official Thailand e-Visa website. Rules can change.
  • Keep a photo and a paper copy of your passport, tickets and hotel bookings.
  • Buy travel insurance.

Money

  • Thailand uses the baht. Carry some cash for markets, street food and small shops.
  • Tell your bank you are travelling abroad and check the charges on your card.

Phone and power

  • Plan mobile data: a local SIM or an eSIM is usually cheaper than roaming.
  • Thai sockets take flat and round two-pin plugs. Carry a universal adapter if your chargers have Indian three-pin plugs.

What to pack

  • Light cotton clothes and comfortable walking shoes that are easy to remove at temples.
  • A scarf or light shirt to cover shoulders, and trousers or a long skirt for temples.
  • Sunscreen, a hat and a refillable water bottle.
  • A light rain jacket between May and October.
  • Any regular medicines, in their original packaging.

Temple etiquette

  • Cover your shoulders and knees, and remove your shoes before entering temple buildings.
  • Do not point your feet at Buddha images, and do not climb on statues.
  • Speak respectfully about the Thai royal family. Disrespect is a serious offence in Thailand.

Getting around safely

  • Agree the fare before getting into a tuk-tuk, or insist on the meter in a taxi.
  • Use your package transfers for airport runs, so you do not negotiate after a long flight.
  • Keep your valuables close in busy markets.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a visa for Thailand from India?

Rules can change, so check the latest requirements on the official Thailand e-Visa website or with the Royal Thai Embassy before you book.

What plug do I need in Thailand?

Thai sockets take flat and round two-pin plugs. Carry a universal adapter if your chargers have Indian three-pin plugs.

What should I wear to temples in Thailand?

Cover your shoulders and knees, and wear shoes that are easy to remove.
