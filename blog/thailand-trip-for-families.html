Thailand trip with family: how to choose a package

The best family package keeps days short, transfers simple and food familiar. Here is how to compare your options.

Get a quote

Published 30 September 2026 · by the Trip to Asia team

A family holiday works best when there is little to organise on the ground and plenty of easy, enjoyable days out. Thailand is well suited to this, if you choose the package carefully.

What to look for in a family package

  • Transfers included: moving between the airport, hotels and sights is the most tiring part with children or parents.
  • Entry fees included: so you do not queue to pay at every stop.
  • Food your family will eat: Indian food is part of every Trip to Asia package (3 meals).
  • Comfortable hotels: our packages use 3-star hotels.
  • Easy days out: attractions like Safari World and Sea Life Bangkok suit children and adults alike.
  • A guide you can follow: a Tamil-speaking guide is available on the packages where listed, with a minimum of 4 members.

Simple tips for travelling with children and parents

  • Keep one slower day after a busy sightseeing day.
  • Carry water, snacks, hats and sunscreen. Thailand is warm through the year.
  • Plan rest time in the afternoon, when the heat is strongest.
  • Keep copies of passports and the hotel address on every phone.
  • Tell us about any health or mobility needs before you travel.
