Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1

Arrival & settle in
  • Arrive in Bangkok and meet your transfer at the airport.
  • Drive to your 3-star hotel and check in.
  • Rest and freshen up.
  • Evening free for a relaxed stroll, street food or a night market.

Day 2

Safari World & Sea Life
  • Safari World: drive-through safari park with animal and dolphin shows.
  • Take your time with the shows and the walk-through park.
  • Sea Life Bangkok Ocean World: tunnels, sharks, rays and thousands of sea creatures.
  • Return to the hotel.

Day 3

Floating Market & city tour
  • Floating Market: take a boat ride and shop among the fruit, snack and souvenir vendors.
  • Bangkok city tour with a Tamil guide (minimum 4 members).
  • Visit the main temples and landmarks on the route.
  • Evening free for shopping or a last look around.

Day 4

Departure
  • Breakfast, pack and check out.
  • Last-minute shopping if time allows.
  • Airport transfer for your onward flight.

*Prices are unconfirmed. Timings and the order of sightseeing may change with traffic and opening days. Indian food covers 3 meals during the stay.
