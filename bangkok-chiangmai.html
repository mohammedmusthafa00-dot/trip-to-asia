Day-by-day itinerary

Day 1

Arrive in Bangkok
  • Arrive in Bangkok and meet your transfer at the airport.
  • Drive to your 3-star hotel and check in.
  • Rest and freshen up.
  • Evening free for a stroll or street food nearby.

Day 2

Bangkok city tour
  • Bangkok city tour with a Tamil guide (minimum 4 members).
  • See the main temples and landmarks of the old city, with entry fees covered.
  • Afternoon and evening free for shopping or rest before tomorrow's travel.

Day 3

To Chiang Mai
  • Breakfast and check out.
  • Transfer to the airport and fly to Chiang Mai.
  • Transfer to your 3-star hotel and check in.
  • Evening at the Walking Street market: handicrafts, snacks and live music.

Day 4

Mae Kampong village
  • Drive up into the hills to Mae Kampong village, a cool, green mountain village known for its waterfall, tea and coffee, and homestays.
  • Walk around the village and enjoy the scenery and fresh air.
  • Return to Chiang Mai in the evening.

Day 5

Temples, museum & food tour
  • Museum and temple visit around Chiang Mai's old city.
  • Free time in the afternoon.
  • Evening authentic Chiang Mai local food tour: taste northern Thai dishes and street snacks.

Day 6

Departure
  • Breakfast, pack and check out.
  • Last-minute shopping if time allows.
  • Airport transfer for your onward flight.

*Prices are unconfirmed. Timings and the order of sightseeing may change with traffic, weather and opening days. Walking Street markets run on set days of the week in Chiang Mai, so the visit will be planned around your dates. Indian food covers 3 meals during the stay.
